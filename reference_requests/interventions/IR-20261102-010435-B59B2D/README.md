# Human Intervention Request IR-20261102-010435-B59B2D

**Task:** V3-008
**Category:** inaccessible-information
**Status:** open

## Summary

V3-008 freeze (plan revision 1 at 655d16a0 and its reviewed successor): independent evidence of the charge dependence of ion lateral spreading (He/C/O in water, 50–430 MeV/u) and independent coverage of the f_dM extrapolation above the 300 MeV proton domain are unavailable, and so are measured C/O ranges in water with uncertainties (row V3); request data access or acceptance of the gaps as recorded.

## Why autonomous resolution is not possible

The only independent physical evidence of the charge dependence of ion lateral spreading would be measured data, and the bounded search (P6 lateral-data search, 19 candidates; P6 measured-range access; P10 single-scattering availability with Geant4 lineage reading and smokes; P11b/P11c extrapolation quantification) found none accessible in numeric form with uncertainties: the measured He/C/O lateral profiles in water (Tessonnier et al. 2017 PMB 62:3958; Schwaab et al. 2011 PMB 56:7813; HIT campaign data "available from the authors", Besuglow 2022) are behind publisher paywalls or held by the measuring groups (EXPERIMENT.md permitted category 1: paper behind inaccessible authentication, data only the operator may be able to obtain). Every single-scattering MC reference available to the autonomous system (Geant4 through OpenTOPAS, G4IonCoulombScatteringModel) shares the screened-Coulomb cross-section lineage of the model under test, so plan revision 1 (amended STOP 4 clause) excludes it as independent evidence; the held-out V5-SS row stays as shared-lineage evidence (branch B). The f_dM extrapolation above 300 MeV proton-equivalent (states up to ≈ 2.5 GeV at the standard points and upper ends) is bounded only within the same Molière theory (row I11-ext). For row V3 the only open candidate for measured C/O Bragg-peak positions (Sihver, Schardt, Kanai 1998) is unusable (curves normalised horizontally to the calculated peaks, figures only, 18O). Autonomous resolution is therefore not possible; integration is not blocked (plan STOP 4 allows integration with the gaps stated in the ledger), but the ledger wording of V2-ION and V3 depends on the answer.

## Exact requested input or action

Unavailable input (1): independent evidence of the charge dependence of lateral spreading for He/C/O in water at 50–430 MeV/u, i.e. measured lateral dose widths or angular spreads with stated uncertainties (candidates Parodi 2013, Schwaab 2011, Tessonnier 2017: no open numeric tables found), or an accessible single-scattering MC reference whose screened-Coulomb cross-section physics is independent of the model used by the exact-Ω mapping; a reference that shares the screened Rutherford law and Molière's Born screening form (including the Geant4 single-scattering configuration of V5-SS, G4IonCoulombScatteringModel, branch B) does not satisfy this input. Unavailable input (2): independent coverage of the f_dM evaluation beyond its 300 MeV proton-qualified domain (proton-equivalent states up to about 2.5 GeV at the standard points and upper ends), declared and bounded only within the same Molière theory (row I11-ext). Unavailable input (3, row V3): measured ranges of carbon and oxygen ions in water, i.e. Bragg-peak and/or R80 positions with stated uncertainties together with the water-equivalent thickness of the beam-line elements upstream of the water, at energies within 80–430 MeV/u (C) and 100–430 MeV/u (O) (candidates: GSI precision Bragg curves (Schardt et al. 2007), HIT numeric tables (Tessonnier 2017, Krämer 2016, Kurz 2012)). Please choose for the lateral input: (A) provide the measured lateral data (e.g. Tessonnier 2017 / HIT σ(z) tables for p, He, C, O in water with uncertainties, or the Schwaab 2011 tables) as files the reference-data tool can acquire; (B) provide, or authorise a route to, an independent-physics single-scattering or angular-distribution reference whose screened-Coulomb cross section is independent of Molière's screened Rutherford law with the Born screening form (not the Geant4 G4IonCoulombScatteringModel / G4eCoulombScatteringModel lineage); (C) accept that V3-008 integrates with ion lateral spreading supported only by theory-derived mapping and implementation checks and by shared-lineage single-scattering MC evidence (V5-SS; no independent evidence), and with the f_dM extrapolation as a declared limitation (a ≤ 1.5 % component of the ±5 % model uncertainty), both recorded as gaps in the ledger. And for the measured-range input: (D) provide measured C/O Bragg-peak or R80 positions in water with uncertainties and the beam-line water-equivalent offsets as files the reference-data tool can acquire; (E) accept that row V3 stays an EVIDENCE GAP in the ledger. Any data provided would be used in a pre-registered amendment row before use, never as construction or calibration.
