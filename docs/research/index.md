# Research reports

Reports produced by scientific research agents for the lead orchestrator on
2026-10-03, before implementation. They are **advisory inputs** to decision
records, not descriptions of implemented behaviour. Each report labels its
statements as verified against primary sources, from memory, or unverified;
decisions cite only what has been verified or is reproduced by committed
scripts.
