# P3 (V3-008): omitted Lindhard-Sorensen term bound, record of 2026-11-01

Status: pre-freeze check, evaluated 2026-11-01 BEFORE the acceptance-plan freeze. The P3 threshold (0.2 % CSDA-range shift trigger; 0.1 % of S expectation) was pre-declared in plan r3. The numbers below are SEEN values and were produced after the threshold was declared; they are an evidence record for the decision, not blind qualification evidence.

## 1. Purpose

Plan check P3 / decision 0043 section 1, LS bullet: bound the stopping-power term that the ionmc analytic Bethe model omits relative to the point-nucleus Lindhard-Sorensen (LS) theory for He, C and O in water, 2-430 MeV/u. ionmc contains Bethe + shell + Barkas + Bloch + first-order Mott (z >= 2) with the Barkas-form effective charge q. The point-nucleus LS correction contains the Bloch term exactly, so the omitted piece is

    r = dL_LS - (Bloch + Mott1).

Rule: expectation r <= 0.1 % of S; if omitting r shifts the CSDA range by more than 0.2 % anywhere in 2-430 MeV/u, an LS option is added (amendment).

## 2. Formula and sources

Convention of ionmc (S/rho = K q^2 (Z/A) beta^-2 [L + ...], "4 pi r_e^2", Mott1 = (pi/2) alpha beta q).

    dL_LS = beta^2/2 + sum_{k>=1} [ k/eta^2 (k-1)/(2k-1) sin^2(d_k - d_{k-1})
                                  + k/eta^2 (k+1)/(2k+1) sin^2(d_{-k} - d_{-k-1})
                                  + k/((4k^2-1)(gamma^2 k^2 + eta^2)) - 1/k ]
    d_k = phi_k - arg Gamma(rho_k + 1 + i eta) + (pi/2)(l - rho_k),
    exp(2 i phi_k) = (k - i eta/gamma)/(rho_k - i eta),  rho_k = sqrt(k^2 - (alpha z)^2),
    l = k (k > 0), l = -k-1 (k < 0),  eta = alpha z / beta.

Sources: Kats, Halenka, Voskresenskaya, arXiv:2008.04819v2, eqs. 9-10 (eq. 10 is the LS correction "as represented in" Lindhard and Sorensen); Rakhno, Mokhov, Striganov (MARS15), arXiv:physics/0505043 (same sum, term by term). Lindhard and Sorensen, Phys. Rev. A 53, 2443 (1996) was NOT read directly; the formula rests on the two secondary reproductions, which agree with each other. Bloch and Mott1 enter in the exact ionmc forms (test `test_forms_match_ionmc` checks equality with the repository functions, constants and masses).

## 3. Checks of the implementation

- Bloch limit (alpha z -> 0 at fixed eta): the summand reduces analytically to the Bloch summand k/(k^2+eta^2) - 1/k (module docstring; tests).
- (pi/2) alpha z beta limit: the large-k summand tail c2/k^2 has c2 equal to the first-order Mott term (fitted, agreement ~1e-8 relative).
- Kats et al. Table 1 (point-nucleus dL_LS, Z = 6-114, beta = 0.85-0.99): all 50 published values reproduced within their 3-decimal rounding plus their truncation Mott1/5000 (`test_against_kats_table1`). These are published theory values; this verifies the implementation, phase convention and Mott sign, not the LS formula itself.
- Complex log-Gamma against |Gamma(1+iy)|^2 = pi y/sinh(pi y), math.lgamma, the arg-Gamma series and the recurrence.
- Golden-value regression table in the tests is SCRIPT-GENERATED by the module (regression only, not a reference).

## 4. Numerics

numpy longdouble (80-bit) phases; complex log-Gamma by recurrence to Re >= 16 plus 8-term Stirling series; l - rho_k computed as (alpha z)^2/(|k|+rho_k). Fitted tail correction (c2/k^2 + c3/k^3 + c4/k^4 at k = K, K/2, K/4, Euler-Maclaurin sums), K doubled until the tail-corrected sums at K and K/2 agree to 1e-11; largest K used 2000, largest change between K/2 and K 1.0e-12. Convergence demonstrations to K = 32000 (16O 2 and 430 MeV/u, 12C 290, p 2 MeV/u) are in the driver report (`--report`): the tail-corrected value is stable to ~1e-11 from K = 2000. Nuclear size is neglected (matters when gamma m_e c R/hbar ~ 1/2; for 16O, R ~ 3 fm, gamma <= 1.47 the ratio is ~0.011).

Evaluation: ionmc water (I = 78 eV), L = S beta^2/(K q^2 Z/A); S changes by (1 + r/L). Range R = int from 2 MeV/u of A dE_u/S with ionmc's exact log-log quadrature on a 400-per-decade grid (941 points incl. the standard points); dR/R = R_ionmc/R_withLS - 1 (positive = ionmc range longer); mm for water of 1 g/cm3, counted from E_cut = 2 MeV/u.

## Mixed charge convention (Codex REVIEW-e0b586fd finding 1)

Chronology: this variant was added on 2026-11-01 after Codex REVIEW-e0b586fd (pre-freeze check), SEEN. The numbers below are not blind evidence. The sections above are unchanged and refer to the variants defined there (effective charge q in LS, Bloch and Mott1; bare z in all three).

Definition. r_mixed = dL_LS(z_nominal) - Bloch(q_eff) - Mott1(q_eff): the LS correction at the bare nuclear charge z, with the ionmc Bloch and first-order Mott terms at the Barkas-form effective charge q exactly as ionmc applies them. r_mixed/S is r_mixed/L in the same normalisation as the other variants (S = K q^2 (Z/A) beta^-2 L, q^2 prefactor as in ionmc and in Geant4). The r_mixed sign is negative at low energy and positive above (12C and 16O change sign between 5 and 10 MeV/u). Above the q/z > 0.999 energy (16.0 and 23.6 MeV/u) the three variants coincide by construction; between 10 MeV/u and that energy the mixed variant differs only slightly from the others (its positive maximum over the grid is 0.057 % of S for 12C and 0.095 % for 16O). The 0.2 % range threshold is crossed (from 2 MeV/u) only for the mixed variant, only for last energies E <= 6.86 MeV/u (12C) and E <= 11.9 MeV/u (16O), where the accumulated range from 2 MeV/u is 0.20 mm (12C) and 0.44 mm (16O) in water; the corresponding absolute shift is about 0.4 and 0.9 micrometre at those energies (0.2 % of the range) and the largest absolute shift over 2-430 MeV/u is 0.141 mm (12C) and 0.185 mm (16O), at the high-energy end, from the 0.05-0.08 % relative shifts there.

Comparison with the reviewer's estimate. At 2 MeV/u the driver gives dL_mixed = -0.04649 (12C) and -0.09469 (16O), against the reviewer's approximately -0.0465 and -0.0947: they agree. The effective-charge variant gives +0.00180 (12C) and +0.00218 (16O) at 2 MeV/u, the bare variant +0.00195 and +0.00232; the large negative value of the mixed convention arises because the LS term is evaluated at z (nominal) while the Bloch term (about -q^2 alpha^2/beta^2 times a sum) and Mott1 are subtracted at the smaller q: LS(z) - Bloch(q) - Mott1(q) = [LS(z) - Bloch(z) - Mott1(z)] + [Bloch(z) - Bloch(q)] + [Mott1(z) - Mott1(q)], the last two brackets being what is missing from the cancellation. The finding is therefore correct in the numbers; the pre-review variants did not compute this combination.

Physics statement (no advocacy). The Lindhard-Sorensen theory is the exact treatment of an electron scattering by a point nucleus of charge z; the Bloch and Mott corrections evaluated at an effective charge q describe a point charge q. For a partially stripped ion below about 10 MeV/u (q < z), the LS theory at the bare charge z and the Bloch/Mott corrections at q are not parts of one consistent theory: the first assumes the full nuclear charge acts at all impact parameters, the second assumes the screened charge does. The mixed convention is the documented indexing of Geant4 (section 7: `Zin = lrint(|charge|)` for the LS table with the q^2 prefactor), that is a lineage convention, not a theorem. The ionmc convention (every correction at q) is the other self-consistent choice, and it is the one implemented in ionmc's Bethe model (primary variant in section 5). The difference between the two conventions is bounded by the numbers above and is confined to the interval where q/z < 0.999 (below 16.0 MeV/u for 12C and 23.6 MeV/u for 16O); in practice it is below 0.1 % of S above 4.69 MeV/u (12C) and 6.58 MeV/u (16O). No claim is made about which convention is closer to the true stopping of a partially stripped ion: that needs a screened-ion LS calculation or experimental data, neither of which is in this check.

- Mixed convention: (a) the 0.2 % CSDA-range trigger, stated as a shift of the range at any domain energy and evaluated with the range counted from E_cut = 2 MeV/u as for the other variants, IS MET (exceeded) for 12C and 16O: max 1.216 % (12C) and 2.545 % (16O), at 2.01 MeV/u, dropping below 0.2 % for end energies above 6.86 MeV/u (12C) and 11.9 MeV/u (16O); it is not met for 4He (max 0.008 %). ## 7. Exploratory cross-check against the Geant4 LS table (SEEN, not construction, not a test)

The research pass downloaded the Geant4 v11.2.0 `G4LindhardSorensenData.cc` (sha256 70ae7c6ba3ef990b7005240bd2b60e4040a3fb60bb1c9e4dbf829c109180eda9; a research download, NOT committed). `p3_driver.py --g4-ls-data PATH` reads it as text only (never compiled or executed) and interpolates linearly in x = ln(gamma-1) where Geant4 uses a cubic spline. Observation: for z = 6 and 8 the un-halved Geant4 `GetDeltaL` agrees with the full point-nucleus dL_LS of this work in the ionmc (4 pi r_e^2) convention across 20-430 MeV/u (absolute difference <= 0.003; <= 5 % where |dL_LS| > 0.02, including the sign change between 20 and 100 MeV/u). For z = 1 and 2 the table tracks neither quantity closely (Geant4 does not use it for p or alpha). The run with the data file reproduced the exploratory table exactly (see logs, section 10).

What the Geant4 11.4.2 `G4LindhardSorensenIonModel.cc` says (untrusted cached file, sha256 e16ed6cd1d132183684ef47948b6f8fe36526a48663a6dc1878d5bb8330eb12c, read with sed/grep only; line numbers from that file):

- L299-300: `res = eloss + CLHEP::twopi_mc2_rcl2*q2*eDensity*(deltaL+deltaL0)*length/beta2;` that is, deltaL is added with prefactor 2 pi m_e c^2 r_e^2 (the "2 pi" convention, a factor 2 below 4 pi r_e^2) times q2 (effective charge-square ratio), electron density and length over beta^2. The multiplier of deltaL is therefore (2 pi r_e^2 m_e c^2 n_e z_eff^2/beta^2) with no factor 0.5 in the expression itself.
- L303 (inside a commented-out debug print): `L = eloss*beta2/(twopi_mc2_rcl2*q2*eDensity*length)`, i.e. the stopping number is normalised with the 2 pi prefactor there. L285 uses the same prefactor (`q2*CLHEP::twopi_mc2_rcl2*eDensity`) for the cut correction in the ICRU-data branch.
- The same function's grep for "0.5", "half", "twopi": 0.5 occurs at L162 (magnetic-moment constant), L263 (`preKinEnergy - eloss*0.5`, mid-step energy), L343, L356 and L372 (secondary-sampling); twopi_mc2_rcl2 at L285 and L300 (and in the debug print L303); `CLHEP::twopi` at L404 (azimuth). There is no halving of deltaL anywhere in this file.
- L230-246: `ComputeDEDXPerVolume` returns the Bragg model (escaled <= fElimit) or the Bethe-Bloch model result; the LS term enters only in `CorrectionsAlongStep` (L255-312, LS branch for escaled > fElimit).
- L157-158 (`charge`, `Zin = G4lrint(std::abs(charge))`): the LS table is evaluated at the nominal particle Z, with q2 from `EffectiveChargeSquareRatio` as prefactor.
- Data table: the table lookup is `G4LindhardSorensenData::GetDeltaL` (included at L54, instantiated at L118). The v11.4.2 `G4LindhardSorensenData.cc` is NOT in the worktree cache. The v11.2.0 file (the SEEN download) shows `GetDeltaL(Z, gamma)`: x = ln(gamma-1), table interpolated between Z rows (1, 10, 18, ...), result multiplied by `g4calc->Z23(Z)` (Z^(2/3)); its header says only "Data on stopping power", created 23.04.2018, with no statement of normalisation.

The source shows that deltaL is multiplied by the 2 pi prefactor; combined with the numerical observation above (un-halved table agrees with the 4 pi-convention dL_LS), the numbers suggest that Geant4 applies half of the LS correction in the 4 pi-convention, but the source alone does not establish the table normalisation, the v11.2.0 vs v11.4.2 data files are not shown to be identical, and the interpolation here differs from Geant4's. This remains exploratory/SEEN. Implication, for attribution only: if confirmed (printed GenericIon dE/dx from the P4 smoke, or the v11.4.2 data file and its derivation), a Geant4-vs-ionmc ion-stopping residual of order dL_LS/2/L (0.36 % of S for 16O at 430 MeV/u) would be partly a Geant4 convention effect; this bears on the I3c attribution diagnostic and on V1, not on the P3 verdict above (the ionmc-side bound r is independent of it).

## 8. Second-order coefficient (informative)

c(beta) = (LS - Bloch - Mott1)/(alpha z)^2 extrapolated to small z is 2.0-2.1 (beta 0.1-0.85), against the Morgan-Eby third-order-Born closed form as quoted in Kats et al. eq. 6 (bracket/2) of 1.70-1.72, i.e. about 20 % higher. Kats et al. Table 1 also lists Morgan-Eby values deviating from LS. Informative only; using Morgan-Eby would make r about 20 % smaller and does not change the verdict.

## 9. Modelling choice and limitations

- Effective charge: the primary variant uses the ionmc effective charge q(E) in LS, Bloch and Mott (as ionmc applies Bloch and Mott); the bare-z variant brackets it (difference <= 0.001 percentage points of S, only below ~10 MeV/u).
- Point nucleus; finite-size correction neglected (ratio ~0.011 for 16O at 430 MeV/u).
- LS 1996 not read directly (section 2); range counted from E_cut = 2 MeV/u.
- The Geant4 comparison uses the v11.2.0 data file and linear interpolation: exploratory.
